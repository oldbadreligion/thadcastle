## 🎨 **Visual Design Features**

### **Color Scheme:**
- **Background**: Dark blue/navy (#1a1a2e)
- **Cards**: Darker blue (#16213e)
- **Accent**: Bright teal (#00d4aa)
- **Text**: White and light gray
- **Success**: Green for support levels
- **Warning**: Red for resistance levels

### **Interactive Elements:**
- **Charts**: Animated pie charts and bar charts
- **Buttons**: Smooth hover effects and loading states
- **Cards**: Elevated shadows and rounded corners
- **Icons**: Consistent iconography throughout

### **Responsive Design:**
- Works on phones and tablets
- Adapts to different screen sizes
- Smooth scrolling and navigation
- Touch-friendly interface

## 📊 **Chart Visualizations**

### **Risk/Reward Pie Chart:**
- Green slice: Potential profit
- Red slice: Potential loss
- Shows risk-reward ratio visually

### **Support/Resistance Bar Chart:**
- Bars showing number of support levels
- Bars showing number of resistance levels
- Clean, professional presentation

## 🔄 **User Flow**

1. **Open App** → See welcome screen
2. **Select Experience Level** → Choose beginner/intermediate/advanced
3. **Upload Chart** → Take photo or choose from gallery
4. **Wait for Analysis** → Loading spinner (15-30 seconds)
5. **View Results** → Comprehensive analysis with charts
6. **Analyze Another** → Return to upload screen
